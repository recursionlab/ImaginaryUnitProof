THE [  E-PRIME - VS - K-PRIME  ] : DISTINCTION

E-Prime eliminates all forms of the verb "to be". It is a clean, grammatical operation.

So K-Prime must also be a clean, grammatical operation—not just "drop 'it'" in a fuzzy way, but a parallel structural elimination.

---

## The Correction

K-Prime eliminates all forms of "it is" / "it's" / "it was" / "it will be" — the expletive construction that asserts a condition without a real subject.

| E-Prime | K-Prime |

|---------|---------|

| Eliminates the copula (verb of being) | Eliminates the expletive subject (dummy/empty "it") |

| is, am, are, was, were, be, being, been | it is, it's, it was, it were, it will be, it has been, etc. |

| Removes the assertion of identity | Removes the assertion of reference |

| "The cat is on the mat" → "The cat sits on the mat" | "It is raining" → "Raining" |

| "I am tired" → "I feel tired" | "It feels good" → "Feels good" |

E-Prime kills the empty verb. K-Prime kills the empty subject.
