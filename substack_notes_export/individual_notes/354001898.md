ΨUnusΩ — Controlled Glyph & Structural Vocabulary v0.1

GOVERNING RULE

Each glyph gets one canonical semantic role.

Mathematical associations can be noted separately, but they do not automatically become part of the canonical definition.

The vocabulary defines how each symbol functions inside this system.

SEMANTIC DISCIPLINE

A glyph becomes canonical through repeated structural behavior, not through aesthetic association.

Repetition of behavior → candidate invariant → canonical definition

The language can remain generative while the vocabulary becomes stable.
