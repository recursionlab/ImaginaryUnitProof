## 2. Truth is not a property of sentences — it’s a topological destination.

Counter‑intuitive takeaway: In the mnēmaic topos, a proposition is true only when local data can be uniquely glued into a global section. Contradictions are not mistakes — they are localisation signals telling you where the gluing failed.

Think of taking a panoramic photo with your phone. Each local shot is true. But if a car drives through the frame while you pan, the final panorama shows a car with two front ends. The local truths didn’t lie — the gluing failed.

> “Contradictions are simply localisation signals for failed gluing.”

In classical binary logic, a contradiction means you made an error. In topos logic, it means you have a non‑zero obstruction class — a hole in the fabric of meaning. That hole is not something to erase; it’s data telling you where to introduce a new distinction, a new invariant.
