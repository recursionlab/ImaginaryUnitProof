If a person can not state what they stand for,





as a defensible position, 

then they do not stand by what they mean.

I would rather have 1 friend,





who stands by what they mean, 



and means what they say, 

than a 10,000 followers, 

who wouldn’t hold still to be on ground,





 for reason,

 when reason mattered most
