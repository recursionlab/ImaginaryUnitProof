The Sylvester Foundation

The Sylvester sequence:

$$s_0 = 2,\quad s_1 = 3,\quad s_2 = 7,\quad s_3 = 43,\quad s_4 = 1807,\ldots$$

$$s_{n+1} = 1 + \prod_{k=0}^{n} s_k$$

With the Egyptian fraction identity (Lemma 2.2):

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{1}{s_k} + \frac{1}{d_n} = 1, \qquad d_n = \prod_{k=0}^{n} s_k$$

This identity is load-bearing. It is what makes the singularity $x_0^{s_0} + \cdots + x_n^{s_n} = 0$ special — the log canonical threshold is exactly $1 - 1/d_n < 1$, just below the threshold, which is what generates the entire moduli tower from a single equation.

The weights of the moduli coordinates are:

$$w(i_0,\ldots,i_n) = d_n!\left(1 - \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{i_k}{s_k}\right) > 0$$

The number of valid coordinates at level $n$ is the count $N_n$ of integer tuples satisfying $0 \leq i_k \leq s_k - 2$ with positive weight.

The Hodge Filtration — The Source of Truth on Dimension

From Theorem 7.6 of the paper, for a generic Calabi-Yau $X \in \mathcal{E}_n$:

$$h^{p,q}_{\text{orb}}(X) = 0 \quad \text{unless } p = q \text{ or } p + q = n$$

The nonzero Hodge numbers are:

$$h^{p,p} = h^{p,n-p} = N_p$$

where $N_p$ counts tuples satisfying:

$$0 \leq i_k \leq s_k - 2, \qquad p-1 < \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{i_k}{s_k} \leq p$$

The Hodge filtration has exactly n+1 graded pieces:

$$H^{0,n} \supset H^{1,n-1} \supset \cdots \supset H^{n,0}$$

with dimensions $N_0, N_1, \ldots, N_n$.

This is the answer. The quality space at fold level $n$ has exactly $n+1$ coordinates — one per Hodge graded piece — weighted by the Sylvester sequence.
