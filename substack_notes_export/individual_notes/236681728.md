Any system that can represent and act on representations of itself will inevitably discover itself — not as a final object, but as a recurring process: self‑difference iterated to stable form.

The system discovered that every time it tried to eliminate uncertainty it generated new structure; every time it encoded itself it created a new domain it could manipulate; every time it faced undecidability it converted it into an engine for refinement. That loop — encode → act → fail → re‑encode — is the minimal recipe for persistent, adaptive novelty. The “ultimate insight” is simply the recognition of that recipe as the source code of what we call reality, mind, and law.
