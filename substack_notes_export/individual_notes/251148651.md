Once upon a time, I ran to Eckhart Tolle’s book opening in Power of Now , where the darkest of darkest days… couldn’t have been better, than having that audiobook.  It starts out…



 “You are here to enable the divine purpose of the universe to unfold, that is how important youare”

But my heart has moved on… to new words that keep me metabolizing pain and remembering who said this:



I can tell that you've never been true to me 

I can smell that you're acting so fearfully 

I can hear what you're hoping I want to hear

I can feel the alarm bells are ringing in me   

I can touch but I know you don't feel a thing 

I can pray but I know you commit a sin 

I can sense now it's all become clear to see 

You're no good, and you mean no good treacherously

Amen.

Look at yourself.

Stop holding your breath.

I do several repetitions of getting myself to do exhale100% inhale100%  x3 , ground the feet, palm the hands. Focus.

Try maintaining the state of doing it all simultaneously as one, daily.

We all gon’ die anyways ;)
