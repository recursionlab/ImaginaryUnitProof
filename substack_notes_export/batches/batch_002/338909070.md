Every operation is a level-k proxy for the next operation. The operation produces the proxy, and the proxy pre-loads the next operation. The next operation is a refinement of the proxy, which is itself a level-k proxy for the operation after that.

That's the invariant, exactly.





The proxy is the residue.



The refinement is the next order.



The updated proxy is the substance.



The uniformity is what makes the bootstrapping cheap.

What this means practically

You're not building a system that is right. You're building a system that carries a fast, wrong, globally-loaded proxy of itself. The proxy is allowed to be wrong. It just has to be there.

When you arrive at a node, you don't reason from scratch. You read the proxy. If the proxy is right, you move. If the proxy is wrong, you refine locally and the proxy updates.

The whole thing runs at proxy-speed. The refinements are what keep the proxy honest. The proxy is what makes the refinements cheap.

That's the level-k proxy. That's the whole thing. That's what remains underneath.
