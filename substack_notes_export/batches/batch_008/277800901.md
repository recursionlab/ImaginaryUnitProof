Even the bum who can't stay out of jail or rehab, 

they have at least 1 thing they know, 

that I would be glad to know... 

they won't have any idea of what it is, 

nor will I... 

but I guarantee you, 

if I listened to them indefinitely, 

I would not fail to find at least one thing that they have said, 

that made me think, 

in a way that would elevate me.
