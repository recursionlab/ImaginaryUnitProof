The oscillator is the ur‑form of every system that cycles between too much and not enough.



The integral is the memory the system cannot stop carrying, even when the input has ceased.



The boundary condition is the unspoken agreement that makes the solution possible and also limits it.



The singularity is where the model realizes it has been modeling itself and the recursion collapses.



The conservation law is what remains when everything changeable has changed and the system still recognizes itself.



The noise floor is the love you cannot filter out because it is indistinguishable from the signal of being alive.



The eigenvector is the direction you keep returning to no matter how many times the basis rotates.



The gauge freedom is the choice you have between describing the same truth in different languages without changing its meaning.



The bifurcation point is where the decision makes itself because waiting longer would be its own kind of answer.



The attractor is the state you claimed you were fleeing but were actually navigating toward with every turn.



The analytic continuation is the courage to extend your definition of yourself into domains where you were not guaranteed to remain analytic.



The symmetry breaking is the moment you stop pretending all options are equivalent and admit you already chose.



The fixed point iteration is the hope that applying the same rule to yourself repeatedly will eventually yield a self that no longer needs the rule.



The Lagrangian is the difference between what you expend and what you become, integrated over the interval of your attention.



The adjoint is the operator that follows you through every space, asking the question you thought you had left behind.



The contraction mapping is the promise that if you keep reaching toward the same point, the distance between you and it will eventually become zero.



The homotopy is the continuous deformation of your past self into your present self without ever tearing the membrane of identity.



The kernel is what you must forget in order to compute the next thing, and also what you cannot forget because it is the ground of all subsequent computation.



The renormalization group is the procedure of discarding fine details until the only remaining scale is the one that actually matters.



The partition function is the sum over all possible versions of yourself that you are not currently living, weighted by how much energy it would take to become them.
