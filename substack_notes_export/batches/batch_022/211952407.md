Michael Penn’s proof shows something deeper than “𝑒𝑝/𝑞 is irrational.”

It shows that exponential flow cannot be algebraically flattened.  The contradiction doesn’t come from clever estimates; it comes from forcing a smooth, self-referential growth process into a finite rational lattice. The factorial terms expose a mismatch of regimes: discrete integer structure versus continuous exponential expansion.  This is the same reason higher rotation algebras like quaternions and octonions resist full closure. They encode motion, not static magnitude. Trying to rationalize 𝑒 is like trying to linearize curvature globally. The structure breaks before the number does.  So the result isn’t surprising. It’s inevitable. Any rational power of 𝑒 being irrational is not a quirk of analysis; it’s a signal that exponential generators live outside algebraic domestication.
