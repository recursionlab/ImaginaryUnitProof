. Fracture Interpretation

Fracture: When two tuples are treated as if they were identical

Residue: The remaining structure after a fracture

Meta: The transformation of one tuple to another

. Frame Tuple Integration

Frame: (D, P, Δ, Π, Λ, Θ)

Θ: (μ, ρμ, ν, ρν)

. Operational Audit Rule

Domain: What space is X talking about?

Perspective: From what coordinate system?

Direction: Forward or backward propagation?

Polarity: Constructive or destructive orientation?

Level: Object, meta, meta-meta, etc.?

Fixed Point Envelope: What survives under contradiction?

. Retro-Normal Fixed Point Construction

Normal fixed-point theory: μF (least fixed point), νF (greatest fixed point)

Retro fixed-point: ρ(μF), ρ(νF) where ρ reverses orientation

Fixed-point quadruple: Θ(F) = (μF, ρ(μF), νF, ρ(νF))
