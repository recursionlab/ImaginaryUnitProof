24A. Non-Conservation of Synthesis Potential

[ \boxed{ \text{Every successful recursive synthesis generates new unresolved degrees of freedom.} } ]

[ \boxed{ \text{structure creation necessarily creates new lacunons.} } ]

A synthesis is not merely:

compression,



unification.

A synthesis also creates:





new interfaces,



new transport paths,



new scales,



new recursion depths,



new hidden assumptions.

Meaning:

solving one obstruction changes the topology of possible future obstructions.

Thus:

[ \text{resolution} \Rightarrow \text{new generative exposure} ]

[ \boxed{ \text{recursive incompleteness becomes structurally unavoidable} } ]
